
# REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY — ROBOT TRADER 2026

## 📋 OFFICIAL POLICY

### 30-DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE

**Policy Statement:**
```
Robot Trader offers a full 30-day money-back guarantee.
If you're not satisfied with your subscription for any reason,
we'll refund your payment in full.

No questions asked. No hassle. Simple and fair.
```

**Scope:**
```
✅ APPLIES TO:
├─ All plans (BASIC, PRO, ENTERPRISE)
├─ New subscriptions (first 30 days)
└─ All payment methods (credit card, bank transfer, etc)

❌ DOES NOT APPLY TO:
├─ Customers who cancel after 30 days (prorated refund below)
├─ Customers who violated Terms of Service
└─ Abuse of refund policy (multiple consecutive refunds)
```

## 📊 REFUND POLICIES BY SCENARIO

### Scenario 1: Customer Within 30-Day Window

**Policy: 100% REFUND**

```
Customer signed up: May 21
Refund request: June 15 (Day 25 of 30-day window)

Refund: €49.00 (BASIC) or €99.00 (PRO)
Processing: Approved immediately
Timeline: 3-5 business days
```

**Process:**
```
1. Customer requests refund
2. Support verifies account
3. Confirm: "Yes, you're within 30-day window"
4. Process refund (no questions)
5. Deactivate account
6. Send confirmation
```

---

### Scenario 2: Customer After 30 Days (Pro-Rata)

**Policy: PRO-RATA REFUND (if canceling mid-month)**

```
Customer's billing cycle: May 21 - June 20
Cancellation request: June 5 (15 days into month)

Days used: 15 / 31 days = 48.4%
Remaining days: 16 / 31 days = 51.6%

Refund: €99 × 51.6% = €51.08
Processing: Approved
Timeline: 3-5 business days
```

**Formula:**
```
Refund = (Days remaining in billing cycle / Total days in cycle) × Monthly fee

Example 2:
- Billing: €99/month
- Canceled on: Day 20 of 30-day cycle
- Days remaining: 10
- Refund: (10/30) × €99 = €33

Next billing: NOT charged (account deactivated)
```

### Scenario 4: Customer Has Had Subscription for 6+ Months

**Policy: STANDARD CANCELLATION (no refund, but pro-rata next month)**

```
Customer signed up: December 1, 2025
Current billing: June 1 - July 1, 2026

BUT: Account deactivated immediately

```

## 🛡️ ABUSE PREVENTION

### Red Flags (Possible Refund Abuse)

```
🚨 SUSPICIOUS PATTERNS:
├─ 3+ refund requests in 6 months
├─ User requests refund immediately after getting paid (trader)
├─ Account shows zero activity but requests refund
├─ User publicly claims "scam" after refund request
└─ Chargebacks filed while requesting refund
```

**Handling Abuse:**

```
IF: User has 2+ previous refunds in 6 months
THEN: 
├─ Investigate usage patterns
├─ Contact user: "We want to help. What's the issue?"
├─ Offer: Alternative (different plan, features, training)
├─ If still requesting refund: Process with note
└─ Future: Require 60-day policy if pattern continues

IF: User files chargeback after refund
THEN:
├─ Contact user immediately
├─ Investigate: Refund processed?
├─ Resolve: If both refund + chargeback, ask user to choose
└─ Report to payment processor (Stripe)
```

## 💬 REFUND CONVERSATION GUIDE

### What NOT to Say

```
❌ "You have to wait 30 days"
   (Sounds like policy > customer)

❌ "You didn't use it enough"
   (Accusatory, not helpful)

❌ "Are you sure?"
   (Pressuring, not supportive)

❌ "That's our policy"
   (Robotic, no empathy)
```

### What TO Say

```
✅ "I'm sorry Robot Trader isn't working for you right now"
   (Empathy first)

✅ "Let me help you get more value"
   (Solution-focused)

✅ "If it's not right for you, here's how to get a refund"
   (Clear, supportive)

✅ "What would have made a difference?"
   (Learning, not judging)
```

### Example Refund Conversation

```
CUSTOMER EMAIL:
"I'd like to cancel. Robot Trader isn't doing what I expected."

SUPPORT RESPONSE:
---
Hi {{NAME}},

Thanks for letting us know, and I'm sorry it didn't meet your expectations.

A few quick questions so I can help:
1. What specific feature were you hoping for?
2. Did you try [feature X]? (many users find value there)
3. Can I help you get more out of it?

If Robot Trader still isn't right for you, I completely understand.
We offer a full 30-day money-back guarantee, so you can get 100% refunded.

Let me know how you'd like to proceed:
1. I help you optimize (schedule: 15-min call)
2. You proceed with cancellation + refund
3. You pause subscription (restart anytime)

What works best for you?

Best,
{{SUPPORT_NAME}}
Robot Trader Support
---
```

## 📈 REFUND METRICS & MONITORING

### Track

```
Weekly:
├─ # refunds requested
├─ # refunds approved
├─ Approval rate (should be >90% within 30 days)
├─ Average refund amount
└─ Time from request to processing

Monthly:
├─ Refund rate (% of customers)
├─ Revenue impact
├─ Churn reasons (categorize)
├─ Win-back candidates (past refunders)
└─ Trending issues (spot patterns)

Target metrics:
├─ Refund rate: < 2% (industry avg 3-5%)
├─ Refund approval rate: 95%+ (for <30 days)
├─ Approval time: < 4 hours
├─ Churn recovery rate: 10-15% (offer something 6 months later)
└─ NPS of refunded customers: >40 (loyalty after refund)
```

### Dashboard Queries

```
SQL-style tracking:

SELECT COUNT(*) as refund_requests
WHERE date >= DATE_SUB(NOW(), INTERVAL 1 WEEK)
GROUP BY status, plan;

→ Identify spikes in refund requests

SELECT AVG(days_before_refund) 
WHERE plan = 'BASIC'
GROUP BY subscription_month;

→ Find which months have earliest refunds
→ Indicates onboarding issues
```
